DailyTaskr – Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2025

Contact: dailytaskr@gmail.com

Quick summary

  • No account. No tracking.
  • Data stays on your device.
  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • Notifications are local (on-device) reminders you control.

Who we are

DailyTaskr is a calm, focused daily planner that lets you add tasks, set priorities and times, and get gentle reminders—without creating an account or sending your task data to our servers.

What data we handle

Data you create (stored on your device)

  • Task details: titles, times, categories, priorities, completion status
  • Reminder settings (e.g., 0 minutes to 1 day before, custom times)
  • App preferences: sorting, filters, notification toggles, haptic/animation settings
  • Widget state for Home Screen widgets

Where it's stored: On your device's local storage. DailyTaskr does not upload your tasks or settings to our servers. Your device backups (e.g., iCloud or computer backups you control) may include DailyTaskr data as part of normal device backup processes.

Data we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, or accounts are required to use the app
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, calendars, photos, or microphone/camera data

When you contact us

If you email dailytaskr@gmail.com, we'll receive the information you include (e.g., your email address and message). We'll use it only to respond and support you, and we'll delete or anonymize those communications when they're no longer needed.

Permissions & how we use them

  • Notifications: Used to deliver local reminders you schedule in the app. You can enable/disable notifications in iOS Settings or per task in the app.
  • Widgets: Home Screen widgets display your tasks and progress locally on your device to help you stay on track.

DailyTaskr does not request access to your contacts, photos, calendars, location, Bluetooth, microphone, or camera.

Children's privacy

DailyTaskr is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided us personal information via email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Data retention & deletion

  • On device: Your tasks and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: Data may persist in your personal device backups according to your backup settings.
  • Support emails: Retained only as long as necessary to address your request.

How to delete your data:

  • Delete tasks within the app, and/or
  • Uninstall DailyTaskr to remove its local data from your device (note: data in your personal backups may remain until those backups are deleted or expire per your settings).

Security

We design DailyTaskr to minimize data collection. Local data benefits from your device's built-in security (e.g., device encryption, passcode). No system is perfect, so we recommend keeping your device updated and protected.

International transfers

We do not transfer your in-app data to our servers. If you email us, your email will be processed by your and our email providers, which may involve international transfer per those providers' standard operations.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EU/EEA, UK, California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. Since the app does not collect in-app personal data on our servers, these rights mainly apply to any information you choose to email us. To exercise rights or ask questions, contact dailytaskr@gmail.com.

CCPA/CPRA (California) notice:

We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the CPRA. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics.

Third-party services

DailyTaskr does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Email communications rely on standard email providers (e.g., Gmail), which process messages according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect improvements or legal requirements. We'll post changes here and update the "Effective date" above. If changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice where appropriate.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns?

  • Data Not Used to Track You: We do not track you across apps or websites.
